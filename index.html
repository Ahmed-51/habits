Nothing is ever pushed on a timer. Edit as much as you like — the chip reads Unsaved and the toolbar button becomes ↑ Push. One press, one commit. You'll be warned if you try to close the tab with work not pushed.

The scoring tiers. Every cell holds one of them and the colour is the whole readout — there is no tick and no letter, and habits are not colour-coded against each other. Greens are credit, reds are not, and the slate one is neither. The names below are only the defaults: in ⚙ Habits each habit can rename every tier and drop the ones it does not need, down to two. The points never move, so however you name them, everything still adds up together.

Not applicable vs. not marked. They score the same zero only while a day is still open. Once a day is over, leaving a cell blank costs you a miss — the app cannot tell that apart from deciding you failed. Not applicable is how you say the habit could not apply that day: it is a real mark, so it holds the cell at zero and no miss is charged. It still counts as one of the day's possible points, so a day of them reads as 0%, not as a skipped day.

Setting one. Click a cell and pick from the menu, or press 1–9 for the habit then 1–6 for the state. A battery segment sets the day to that many passes (clicking the topmost lit one un-fills it), and the big button passes or clears the whole day.

Everything counts in points. The battery, the percentages, the week card and the comparison all add these values up, out of one point per habit per day. A full-battery day still means every habit at Pass.

The weekly target. The battery is always today — that doesn't change. The week card underneath counts every point across the whole week against one target, so a bad Tuesday doesn't write off the week: a strong Saturday can still cover it. Click the target number to change it, or the date range to switch between Monday and Sunday starts. Weeks spanning two months are handled; each day counts against its own month's habit list.

Sync (optional). The chip in the toolbar says Local only until you connect a private GitHub repo — click it to set that up. Once connected, edits push a couple of seconds after you stop clicking, and any browser you unlock pulls the same data. It still works offline and catches up later. Leave it off and nothing changes.

Storage. Everything is written to this browser's local storage the moment you click, so nothing is lost if you close the tab. Save writes a habits.json backup — put that inside your Google Drive folder and Drive keeps it synced across machines. Load brings it back.

Months. Each month stores its own snapshot of the habit list, so editing this month's habits never rewrites your history. New months start from your current list.
